# Intake: facts and open questions for Nico Agustin's portfolio

## How gaps work

- Every fact on the site lives in `lib/site.ts` or `content/projects.ts`, and each one comes from a source listed below.
- An unknown fact is **left out**. It is never shown as a placeholder, a "coming soon" or an empty heading: the field stays `undefined` and the section simply doesn't render.
- To fill a gap, answer the question below (a sentence is enough). The orchestrator adds the answer to the data file, and the section appears.
- Never published, whatever the source: phone and street address (city only: Malolos, Bulacan, Philippines), customer or lead data, and the reporting app's own brand name.
